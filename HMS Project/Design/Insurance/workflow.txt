1. System Scope
• Supports SHA, private insurance companies, TPAs, corporate medical schemes, individual medical policies and contracted
healthcare providers.
• Core lifecycle: Payer & scheme setup → Member management → Eligibility → Benefit verification → Pre-authorization →
Service utilization → Claim intake → Validation → Adjudication → Query/rejection → Appeal/resubmission → Payment →
Allocation → Reconciliation → Closure.
2. User Roles
• System Administrator — system configuration, users and permissions.
• Insurance Administrator — payer, scheme, benefit, tariff and rule configuration.
• Membership Officer — member registration, amendments, dependants and eligibility.
• Pre-Authorization Officer — authorization requests, review and decisions within limits.
• Claims Officer — claim intake, validation, queries and resubmission.
• Claims Adjudicator — coverage, tariff, benefit and financial adjudication.
• Claims Manager — escalations, high-value claims, overrides and final approvals.
• Appeals Officer — appeal preparation, submission and tracking.
• Finance Officer — payment batches, remittance, allocation and reconciliation.
• Finance Manager — payment approvals, write-offs, variances and financial adjustments.
• Management/Reporting User — dashboards and management reports.
• Auditor — read-only access and audit review.
• Provider Portal User — provider-side claim and authorization interaction.
3. Payer / Insurance Company Setup
• Workflow: Payer registration → Contract setup → Scheme setup → Benefit setup → Tariff setup → Integration setup →
Activation.
• Fields: Payer ID/code, payer name, payer type, registration/tax details, contacts, claims/finance contacts, payment terms,
API/EDI availability, portal availability, integration details, effective dates and status.
4. Scheme / Policy Setup
• Hierarchy: Insurance Company → Scheme → Policy → Principal Member → Dependants.
• Configuration: scheme/policy number, policy period, annual limit, OPD/IPD limits, maternity/dental/optical limits, room limit,
copay, deductible, exclusions, authorization rules, provider network, tariff and payment terms.
5. Member Management
• Workflow: New member → Registration → Policy assignment → Benefit assignment → Activation.
• Statuses: DRAFT, ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, EXPIRED, CANCELLED, DECEASED, TERMINATED.
• Fields: Member ID, member number, policy number, scheme, principal member, relationship, employer, effective/expiry
dates, status, benefit utilization and available balance.
6. Eligibility Verification
• Workflow: Member number → Verify membership → Check policy → Check effective date → Check status → Check benefit
→ Eligibility result.
• Verification channels: API, EDI, portal or manual verification.

• Results: ELIGIBLE, NOT_ELIGIBLE, INACTIVE, EXPIRED, NOT_FOUND, PENDING, MANUAL_VERIFICATION.
• Failure reasons: inactive membership, expired policy, suspended member, exhausted benefit, member not found or
excluded service.
7. Benefit Verification
• Workflow: Member → Scheme → Benefit → Limit → Utilization → Available balance.
• Benefit decision: Covered, partially covered, not covered or authorization required.
• Controls: annual limits, service limits, frequency limits, copay, deductible, exclusions and remaining balance.
8. Pre-Authorization
• Workflow: Authorization request → Eligibility check → Benefit check → Service/clinical review → Cost review → Decision.
• Decisions: APPROVED, PARTIALLY_APPROVED, REJECTED.
• Statuses: DRAFT, SUBMITTED, PENDING, APPROVED, PARTIALLY_APPROVED, REJECTED, CANCELLED,
EXPIRED, CLOSED.
• Fields: Authorization ID/number, member, payer, scheme, provider, service/procedure, diagnosis/justification, quantity,
estimated amount, attachments, urgency, validity dates and external reference.
9. Service Utilization
• Workflow: Authorized service → Service used → Actual cost → Benefit utilization → Member balance update.
• Controls: authorized vs utilized quantity, authorized vs actual amount, remaining authorization and remaining member
benefit.
10. Claim Intake
• Claim sources: hospital/provider, pharmacy, laboratory, doctor, dental provider, optical provider and other contracted
providers.
• Workflow: Provider service → Claim created → Member linked → Authorization linked → Benefit linked → Invoice linked.
• Claim fields: claim ID/number, provider, member, payer, scheme, policy, authorization, service period, invoice, claim
amount, documents and submission reference.
11. Claim Validation
• Validation sequence: Member → Policy → Eligibility → Authorization → Benefit → Tariff → Duplicate → Documents →
Amount.
• Outcomes: VALIDATED or VALIDATION_FAILED.
• Typical errors: invalid member, expired policy, missing/invalid authorization, benefit exhausted, incorrect tariff, duplicate
claim, missing documents or invalid amount.
12. Claim Adjudication
• Workflow: Validated claim → Coverage → Benefit → Tariff → Authorization → Rule/clinical check → Duplicate/fraud
controls → Financial calculation → Decision.
• Decisions: APPROVED, PARTIALLY_APPROVED, REJECTED.
• Calculation: Gross claim → contractual adjustment → non-covered amount → benefit limit adjustment → copay →
deductible → insurance liability.
13. Claim Query Management

• Workflow: Claim → Query → Reason → Assign → Provider response → Document upload → Review → Resolve/reject →
Resubmit.
• Statuses: OPEN, ASSIGNED, AWAITING_PROVIDER, RESPONSE_RECEIVED, UNDER_REVIEW, RESOLVED,
REJECTED.
• Fields: Query ID, claim, reason, amount affected, assigned user/department, query date, due date, response, documents,
resolution and status.
14. Claim Rejection & Resubmission
• Workflow: Rejected → Rejection reason → Provider notification → Correction/appeal → Internal review → Resubmission →
Adjudication.
• Reasons: invalid member, expired policy, exhausted benefit, excluded service, no authorization, authorization exceeded,
incorrect tariff, duplicate claim, missing documentation or data/coding error.
15. Appeals
• Workflow: Rejected/disputed claim → Appeal raised → Supporting documents → Internal review → Appeal submitted →
Payer review → Decision.
• Outcomes: APPEAL_APPROVED, APPEAL_PARTIALLY_APPROVED, APPEAL_REJECTED.
16. Payment Processing
• Workflow: Approved claims → Payment batch → Payment approval → Payment instruction → Bank payment → Payment
confirmation → Remittance advice → Allocation.
• Payment statuses: DRAFT, APPROVAL_PENDING, APPROVED, PROCESSING, PAID, PARTIALLY_ALLOCATED,
FULLY_ALLOCATED, RECONCILED.
17. Payment Allocation
• Workflow: Payment received → Remittance → Match claim numbers → Match amounts → Allocate → Reconcile.
• Outcomes: FULL_MATCH, PARTIAL_MATCH, OVERPAYMENT, UNDERPAYMENT, UNALLOCATED.
18. Reconciliation
• Three-way match: Approved Claim ↔ Remittance Advice ↔ Bank Payment.
• Results: Reconciled, variance identified, unallocated or pending investigation.
• Variance reasons: contractual deduction, benefit adjustment, tariff difference, disputed amount, administrative deduction or
other configured reason.
19. Complete Status Lifecycle
• Member: DRAFT → ACTIVE → SUSPENDED → EXPIRED → TERMINATED.
• Authorization: DRAFT → SUBMITTED → PENDING → APPROVED/PARTIALLY_APPROVED/REJECTED →
EXPIRED/CANCELLED → CLOSED.
• Claim: DRAFT → VALIDATING → VALIDATION_FAILED/READY_FOR_ADJUDICATION → UNDER_REVIEW →
QUERY/APPROVED/PARTIALLY_APPROVED/REJECTED → APPEAL/RESUBMITTED → PAYMENT_PENDING →
PARTIALLY_PAID/PAID → RECONCILED → CLOSED.
• Payment: DRAFT → APPROVAL_PENDING → APPROVED → PROCESSING → PAID →
PARTIALLY_ALLOCATED/FULLY_ALLOCATED → RECONCILED.
20. Approval Rules
• Approval limits must be configurable by payer, scheme, transaction type and amount.

• High-value authorizations and claims require escalation according to configured limits.

24. Notifications
• Authorization: submitted, approved, rejected and expiring.
• Claims: ready, submitted, query, rejected, resubmission due, approved and payment received.
• Finance: payment received, variance, unallocated payment and overdue receivable.
• Channels: in-system notification, email and SMS where configured.
25. Management Reports
• Claims: submitted, approved, rejected, queried, pending, paid, outstanding, aging, by payer, by scheme, by provider and
rejection analysis.
• Membership: active members, expired members, new members, dependants and benefit utilization.
• Finance: insurance receivables, payment reconciliation, unallocated payments, payment variance, outstanding balances
and write-offs.
• Authorization: pending, approved, rejected, high-value, expiring and utilization vs authorization.
26. SHA & Private Insurance Workflow
• Common lifecycle: Eligibility → Benefits → Authorization → Claim → Validation → Adjudication → Payment →
Reconciliation.
• SHA and private payer integrations can use different technical methods. Private insurers/TPAs may use API, EDI, portal,
secure file, email or manual workflows.
• Payer-specific tariffs, benefits, limits, authorization rules and integration mappings must be configurable rather than
hard-coded.
27. Key Business Rules
• A claim cannot proceed to adjudication if mandatory validation fails.
• Authorization must be linked when the scheme requires it.
• Claims cannot exceed configured benefit/authorization rules without an approved override.
• Duplicate claim detection must run before adjudication.
• Every query and rejection must have a reason code.
• Payment allocation must retain a full audit trail.
• Partially paid claims remain outstanding until resolved or formally adjusted.
• Every financial adjustment must record reason, approver and timestamp.
• Closed claims cannot be edited; corrections use controlled adjustment/resubmission workflows.
• Payer-specific tariffs, benefits, limits and authorization rules must be effective-date driven.
