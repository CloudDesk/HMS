## Source gaps and ambiguous artifacts

- The full Ophthalmology source FSD referenced by the understanding document and alignment matrices was not located. Its interpretation, contracts and plans are not relabelled as an approved source FSD.
- The Release 2 source exists at `docs/HMS_Release2_FSD.docx` in the ignored working tree; older documents referring to absent `doc/HMS_Release2_FSD.docx` are preserved unchanged. The different path and historical absence claim are recorded rather than silently corrected.
- The Release 1 scope document labels advanced M-Pesa/insurance integrations, patient portal and native mobile as later-release work. Such mentions do not establish complete FSD coverage for those modules.
- Explicit source statuses such as proposed/review/draft/completed are not independent business or clinical approval. No signed acceptance or accountable approval record was identified.
- Some source reports mix implementation notes, checks and manual scenarios. They are assigned one primary category; embedded material remains intact and counts are not duplicated across categories.
- Raw logs with concrete failed checks are retained as failed historical evidence, not success. Command-only/truncated logs without a completed outcome are withheld from Test Result.
- Existing UI prototypes were originally collected as Design references, not application screenshots or acceptance evidence. Their HTML/image copies have now been removed under the format policy; original sources remain unchanged. Retained Markdown may describe those historical prototypes.
- Binary Word source text was inspected through OOXML; exact bytes were copied and hashed. Document layout was not changed or re-rendered. The pre-existing PNG contact sheet/source manifests were inspected; no exhaustive OCR/security guarantee is claimed for image pixels.
- Vendor/generated build directories, caches, scratch checkouts, Git internals and runtime patient storage are outside the source-document corpus. Patient uploads and credential CSVs were intentionally not ingested. Archives/backups were not unpacked or treated as specifications. No inaccessible ref was encountered.
- Repository ignore rules hide `.docx` and `.log` copies from ordinary git status. They still exist and are hash-verified in the working tree; no ignore configuration or index was modified. The inventory lists them explicitly.
