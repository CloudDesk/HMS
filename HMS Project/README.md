## Reading Rules & Governance

- **Original Sources**: Exact copies preserved without editing. FSD approval remains UNVERIFIED unless separately confirmed by the owner. Shared requirements are stored once under Cross-Module HMS. Filenames with `__<hash>` retain conflicting versions without overwriting. Design permits Markdown/text only.
- **Generated FSD Drafts**: Generated for each of the 45 modules under `HMS Project/FSD/<Module>/<Module>_FSD.md`. All drafts are explicitly marked `Draft — Pending Review`. They synthesize documented specifications and implementation observations into 359 unique functional requirements (`FR-*`). They distinguish confirmed requirements from implementation observations; business approval is unverified.
- **Safety**: No branches were switched or modified, no fetch/merge/rebase/reset/cherry-pick/push/commit occurred, no tests were executed, and no application/configuration/dependency/database changes were made. Pre-existing root M-Pesa audit remains unchanged.
