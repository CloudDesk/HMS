1. Document Purpose and Scope Basis
This document defines the functional scope for HMS Phase 2 development. Phase 2 is planned to implement the items that were not included in the orange-marked Phase 1 OPD scope on the manager-reviewed whiteboard.
Phase 1 remains the baseline for OPD patient registration, booking, vitals, consultation, e-prescription, lab, scan, basic billing and patient EMR timeline. Phase 2 shall extend the application into the additional flows identified on the whiteboard, mainly IP beds, surgery/procedure booking, emergency handling, consent attachment, bed availability/allotment and advance payment handling.
Important implementation rule
Phase 2 should reuse the same patient record, encounter/visit context, doctor availability rules, billing permission model and EMR timeline behavior already established in Phase 1. New Phase 2 activities must not create disconnected patient records or isolated module entries.

2. Phase 2 Scope Boundary
Area
Phase 2 Scope Boundary
Primary scope
Extend the HMS from OPD-only workflow into emergency, surgery/procedure scheduling and IP bed/admission conversion workflows.
Patient record rule
All Phase 2 workflows must connect to the same patient profile, EMR timeline, documents and relevant visit/encounter.
Billing rule
Billing is permission-driven. The role performing billing must be controlled through Admin permissions and must not be hard-coded to Reception or any fixed user type.
Configuration rule
Admin controls masters and configuration for IP beds, surgery/procedure services, consent forms, department setup, advance payment rules and authorized screen actions.

3. Phase 2 Functional Scope Summary
IP bed configuration and bed availability management.
Reception-led bed checking, bed allotment and admission conversion support.
Doctor-driven surgery/procedure referral and department selection with surgery date capture.
Surgery/procedure booking through Reception based on doctor availability, department availability and bed availability where required.
Consent form attachment for surgery/admission-related flows.
Advance payment capture and allocation before surgery/IP bed allotment where configured.
Emergency patient flow with downstream pharmacy, lab and scan handling.
Emergency disposition: discharge from emergency or convert to IP through Reception/admission workflow.
Pharmacy, lab and scan request processing for Phase 2 sources such as emergency, surgery/procedure and IP/admission workflows.
Payment status visibility across pharmacy, lab, scan, surgery/procedure, emergency and admission-related services.
Reports for emergency register, surgery/procedure schedule, bed occupancy, admission conversion, advance payment and pending payment/discharge status.
Permission-based access, audit trail and connected EMR timeline updates for all Phase 2 activities.

11. Surgery / Procedure Booking Scope
Scope interpretation
With surgery date and department, plus Reception booking with bed check, consent form and advance payment. This document treats Phase 2 as surgery/procedure booking and operational tracking. Full OT/anesthesia/nursing intra-operative documentation is listed as To Be Confirmed unless separately approved.

11.1 Surgery / Procedure Booking Flow
1. Doctor recommends surgery/procedure and selects department/service.
2. System creates surgery/procedure referral linked to patient and active encounter/visit.
3. Reception opens referral request and checks doctor/service availability.
4. Reception selects proposed surgery/procedure date and time if available.
5. System validates no doctor schedule conflict and no duplicate active surgery booking for same patient/service.
6. Reception checks bed requirement and bed availability where surgery requires admission/bed.
7. Reception attaches required consent form or marks consent pending.
8. System checks whether advance payment is required.
9. Authorized billing user records advance payment if required.
10. Reception confirms surgery/procedure booking and allotment status.
11. Patient EMR timeline is updated with booking, consent, payment and allotment information.
Scenario
Required System Behaviour
Doctor refers patient for surgery
Create surgery/procedure referral and make it visible to Reception/authorized users.
No doctor availability
Do not confirm booking; show alternative available dates/times.
No bed available
Allow waiting list/hold status if configured; do not allot unavailable bed.
Consent missing
Block confirmation if consent is configured as mandatory; otherwise show pending consent warning.
Advance payment missing
Block booking/allotment if payment is configured as mandatory; otherwise mark advance pending.
Surgery date changed
Reschedule booking and maintain audit trail with old/new date and reason.
Patient cancels procedure
Cancel booking, release bed/slot if held and update billing/advance payment status based on policy.
Doctor cancels procedure
Mark impacted booking and trigger reschedule/cancel workflow.

13. Emergency Workflow Scope
Emergency source note
Emergency -> Pharmacy, Lab, Scans -> Discharge / convert to IP -> Receptionist. Phase 2 shall therefore include emergency workflow with downstream service routing and disposition to discharge or IP conversion.

13.1 Emergency Flow
1. Emergency patient arrives or is created as emergency case.
2. Reception/Emergency user searches existing patient or creates minimum emergency record if details are incomplete.
3. Emergency encounter is created with priority and source details.
4. Doctor/Emergency clinician evaluates patient and records emergency notes/disposition.
5. Doctor orders pharmacy, lab and/or scan services if required.
6. Pharmacy/Lab/Scan process their requests and update same emergency encounter.
7. Billing/payment status is updated for emergency services by authorized billing user.
8. Doctor marks final disposition: Discharge from emergency or Convert to IP.
9. If discharged, emergency bill/payment and summary are completed and EMR timeline is updated.
10. If converted to IP, Reception completes bed availability, consent, advance payment and bed allotment workflow.
Emergency Scenario
Required System Behaviour
Patient identity unknown/incomplete
Allow minimum data registration with temporary/emergency identifier and mark profile completion pending.
Existing patient found
Link emergency encounter to existing patient record.
Pharmacy/lab/scan required
Create downstream requests linked to emergency encounter.
Emergency discharged
Mark disposition as discharged, complete billing/payment status and update timeline.
Emergency converted to IP
Route to Reception/admission workflow for bed allotment and advance payment if required.
Patient leaves before closure
Mark status as left/abandoned with reason and pending billing status if applicable.

14.1 Pharmacy Inventory, E-Prescription Billing and Stock Deduction
The Pharmacy workflow shall include inventory-linked billing from the doctor-generated e-prescription. The e-prescription must be visible to the Pharmacy user and must be usable as the source for bill creation and medicine dispensing.
Required flow
Doctor creates an e-prescription from the consultation workflow.
The e-prescription is displayed in the Pharmacy work queue/list under the same patient and encounter/visit context.
Pharmacy opens the e-prescription and reviews all medicine line items before billing or dispensing.
Pharmacy shall be able to create the pharmacy bill directly from the e-prescription.
The system shall check pharmacy inventory before final bill/dispense confirmation.
Once the bill/dispense action is confirmed, the final dispensed quantity shall be deducted from Pharmacy inventory stock.
The deduction shall update the medicine/item stock balance and remain linked to the patient, visit/encounter, prescription and bill.
Editable pharmacy fields
Pharmacy shall be able to edit the medicine/item name before final billing/dispensing where substitution or correction is required.
Pharmacy shall be able to edit quantity before final billing/dispensing.
Pharmacy shall be able to edit other prescription/billing line fields required for dispensing, such as unit, batch, dose/instructions, rate/amount and remarks, based on configured permissions.
All edits made by Pharmacy to the e-prescription billing line shall be saved with audit details, including user, date/time and changed values where applicable.
Inventory deduction rules
Stock deduction must happen based on the final confirmed quantity, not merely the quantity originally prescribed by the doctor.
If Pharmacy increases the quantity, the system must validate available stock before allowing billing/dispensing.
If Pharmacy reduces the quantity, stock shall be deducted only for the reduced final dispensed quantity.
If the selected quantity is not available, the system shall block final dispensing or allow partial dispensing only if the configured pharmacy policy permits it.
Cancelled or unconfirmed e-prescriptions shall not deduct stock.
If a pharmacy bill or dispensing action is reversed/cancelled, inventory reversal shall be controlled by permission and audit log.
